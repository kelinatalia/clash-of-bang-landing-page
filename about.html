ABOUT LEGENDS

Discover the history and the visionaries behind the ultimate Dark Elixir experience.

Our Mission

"To create the world's most inclusive and competitive Dark Elixir gaming community, where players of all skill levels can learn, compete, and grow together while celebrating the strategic depth and excitement of the game we love."

Community Milestones

2018

Foundation

Clash of BaNG was founded by a group of passionate gamers who shared a vision of creating the ultimate Dark Elixir gaming community.

2019

First Tournament

Hosted our inaugural clan tournament with over 50 participating clans, establishing ourselves as a competitive gaming hub.

2020

Community Growth

Reached 10,000 active members and launched our Discord server, creating a vibrant space for strategy discussions and clan coordination.

2021

International Expansion

Expanded globally with regional chapters across Europe, Asia, and the Americas, bringing together players from over 40 countries.

2022

Championship Series

Launched the annual Dark Elixir Championship Series with a prize pool of $100,000, attracting top-tier competitive players worldwide.

2023

Content Creation Hub

Established our content creation program, supporting streamers and YouTubers with resources to grow the community presence.

2024

Mobile Platform Launch

Released our companion mobile app for clan management, strategy guides, and real-time tournament updates.

2025

Community Milestone

Celebrated reaching 100,000 members and hosted our largest tournament yet with participants from 75 countries.

2026

Innovation Era

Introduced AI-powered strategy analytics and launched our mentorship program pairing veteran players with newcomers.
